WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT SAY

Evidence, not a guarantee.

  • NU7 Testnet activation height: 4,465,026.
  • NU7 consensus branch ID: 0x77190AD9.
  • A reported height at or above activation does not prove the node's software or mempool accepts every NU7 transaction.
  • Browser CORS, authentication, and endpoint protocol can prevent a probe even when a node is running.
  • Use Testnet endpoints only. This tool does not broadcast transactions.
